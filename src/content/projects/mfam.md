The project enabled an interactive digital model for heritage enhancement, based on an online platform that integrates audiovisual and immersive content (through virtual reality) focused on the community life of three pilot villages in the Mountain Villages Network (Videmonte, Cabeça, and Alvoco das Várzeas). The project combines technology, applied research, and design centered on creating a storytelling narrative focused on the stories of local communities, ancestral ways of doing things, and the resources of the territory, presenting them as possibilities for the future.
