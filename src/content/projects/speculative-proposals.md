Speculative Proposals is an exploratory pedagogical resource developed to integrate speculative design into higher education, encouraging design students to question established assumptions, challenge conventional perspectives, and explore alternative future scenarios. The project investigates how structured creative tools can support critical thinking, imagination, and the exploration of possible social, cultural, and technological transformations.

Initially developed and applied in 2023 at the Faculty of Architecture of the University of Lisbon, the resource introduces a card-based system organised around four dimensions: Who, What, Where, and When. Through the combination of different actors, situations, contexts, and time horizons, participants are encouraged to construct hypothetical scenarios, examine their implications, and translate them into speculative design proposals.

Since its initial application, Speculative Proposals has been explored across different thematic areas and university contexts, adapting to diverse pedagogical objectives and approaches to design education. These experiences have informed successive iterations of the resource, introducing new provocations, constraints, and possibilities for exploration. This ongoing process of experimentation and refinement contributes to investigating the pedagogical potential of speculative design as a means of fostering critical reflection, challenging dominant narratives, and imagining alternative futures.
