Fashion e-commerce continues to struggle with high return rates driven by limited product information and the inability for consumers to physically assess garments. These returns generate significant environmental impact and expose gaps in the way digital product presentations communicate essential attributes. Visual Interaction Systems for Transparent AI in Fashion E-commerce (VISTA) investigates how Visual Assistance Tools (VAT), including augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR), and emerging AI-assisted visualisation systems, can support clearer, more reliable product understanding and promote more confident, sustainable purchasing decisions.
