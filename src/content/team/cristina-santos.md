Cristina is Assistant Professor at the Polytechnic Institute of Beja. She holds a Bachelor's degree in Industrial and Product Design, a Master's degree in Graphic Design, and a PhD in Design from the Lisbon School of Architecture. 

She has worked as an industrial and graphic designer for several companies. 

She teaches graphic design, information design, arts, and multimedia.
