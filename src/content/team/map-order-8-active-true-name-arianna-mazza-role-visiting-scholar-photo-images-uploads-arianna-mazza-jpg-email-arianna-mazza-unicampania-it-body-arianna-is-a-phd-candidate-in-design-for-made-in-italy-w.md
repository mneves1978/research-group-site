Arianna is a PhD candidate in Design for Made in Italy, with a research focus on sustainability, circular economy and interaction design. 

Her work explores how complex sustainability frameworks can be translated into accessible tools for craftspeople, designers and small businesses. Through a practice-oriented approach, she investigates the relationship between traditional craftsmanship, situated knowledge and digital technologies. 

Her current research focuses on interactive tools that support the understanding of sustainability requirements and encourage reflection on materials, production processes and product life cycles.
