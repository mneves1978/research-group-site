Marco is an Associate Professor at the Lisbon School of Architecture – ULisbon, where he lectures in master’s and doctoral degrees. He coordinates the Interaction Design Master’s degree and Design, Interaction, and Visualization research group. Marco holds a PhD and a Habilitation in Design from the University of Lisbon. 

His research focuses on design research methods, design for interactions, experiences with print and digital media, interaction transitions to static media through technological augmentation, and understanding of tangible interfaces. He also works on several intersections between interaction design, social challenges, educational settings, and information visualization.
